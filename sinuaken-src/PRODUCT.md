## Platform

web

## Users
Residents of Narva and Ida-Virumaa (Sillamäe, Jõhvi, Kohtla-Järve, Kiviõli), mostly Russian-speaking, living in Soviet-era panel apartment blocks (typical window openings) and private houses. Also apartment associations (korteriühistu) renovating whole buildings. Most arrive from a Google Ads search on a phone ("окна Нарва"). (Inferred from the live site, the ad and the region.)

## Product Purpose
Marketing site for SinuAken (Buono Invest OÜ), PVC/wooden windows, balcony glazing, window repair, exterior doors, stretch ceilings, mosquito nets. Goal: the visitor finds their window type, sees the all-in price, and sends a request or calls.

## Positioning
Fixed all-in prices for the standard window sizes of Narva apartments: price includes window, installation, PVC sill, drip edge, side reveals, interior finishing, removal and disposal of the old window. Two lines: CLASSIC and PERFECT. Free visit of a master and measurement on the day of request (from the Google ad).
