Common questions

If yours is not here, just message us

How much does a set cost?

As a guide for BC Forged: monoblock from €4,760, two-piece from €6,030 per set. The price goes up with diameter, width and finish complexity. Prices include VAT and delivery within Estonia. We give the exact total after the fitment check, before you pay anything

Why does it take so long?

The set is forged and machined for your car: size, offset, bolt pattern, centre bore and finish. It is made to order, not pulled from a warehouse

How do I pay?

50% once you approve the sketch and drawing, the rest before the set leaves the factory. The wheels are made individually, so the order cannot be cancelled once production starts

Will I pass the vehicle inspection?

BC Forged wheels pass the Japanese JWL and VIA strength tests. Made-to-order wheels have no German TÜV certificate: only series-production models get one. The Estonian inspection checks fitment: the tyre suits the wheel, nothing rubs, the tread stays inside the arch and the bolts or nuts are correct. We choose the specs for your car; the result depends on correct fitting

What about the warranty?

BC Forged gives 3 years on the structure and 1 year on the finish. If something is wrong, send a photo and your order number and we deal with the supplier

Will they clear my brakes?

We ask about your brakes in the request and check the caliper clearance. For carbon ceramic and aftermarket brakes we choose the spoke profile separately

Where do I get them fitted?

At our partner tyre shops in Tallinn, with balancing and the correct bolts or nuts. We can also pick tyres for the new wheels
