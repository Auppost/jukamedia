Questions people actually ask

How long will it take?

Websites take 3-10 days, online stores 3-4 weeks. The clock starts when I have your materials - waiting on your side doesn't eat the timeline.

What if I don't like the result?

Two revision rounds are included in every project, and we agree on the design direction before I build. You won't see the result for the first time at the end.

Who owns the site and the domain?

You do. Domain, hosting and every account are registered in your name from day one. If we part ways, everything stays with you.

Do you work with my country and language?

Clients across the EU, Canada and the US. I work in English, Russian and Estonian, and deliver websites in any language your customers speak.

I already have a website. Do I have to start over?

No - often that's an advantage. The audit is free, and a redesign costs 15% less than building from scratch. I only suggest redoing what actually blocks results.

Why is it cheaper than an agency?

An agency's price includes an office, account managers and three rounds of internal approvals. Mine includes one person doing the work. Same result, less overhead.

How does payment work?

50% upfront, 50% on delivery, with an official invoice from an Estonian (EU) company. Monthly services are billed month to month.

What if I want to leave?

You say so, and you leave. No long-term contracts, no exit fees - and everything already belongs to you anyway.
