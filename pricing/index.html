Terms

Payment

50% upfront, 50% on delivery. Projects under $350 are paid in full upfront.

Revisions

Two rounds of revisions are included. Further changes are billed at the hourly rate.

Timelines

Counted from the moment all materials are in: copy, photos, access. Waiting on your side doesn't eat the timeline.

What stays yours

Source files, all access, domain and hosting are registered to you. You can switch contractors at any moment without losing anything.

Not included

Domain and hosting ($15-70 per year), paid photos and fonts, ad budgets. All paid directly and at cost.

Why it costs less than a studio

A studio charges three to four times more for the same work, because its price includes an account manager, an office and three rounds of approvals. Here you talk directly to the person doing the work.
