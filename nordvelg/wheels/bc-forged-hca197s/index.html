About the HCA197

The centre is made of many slim spokes woven into a mesh, in the spirit of classic racing wheels. The barrel is a separate part, so you can have a visible lip and paint the centre and barrel in different colours. The photo shows a dark centre with a bronze barrel.

It is a two-piece wheel: the centre bolts to the barrel. Diameters run from 18 to 23″ and widths up to 14J, so the HCA197 works on wide rear axles too. There are two versions, HCA197 and HCA197S, compared below. The design looks right on the Ferrari 296 GTB, Purosangue and 12Cilindri.
