About the EH172

Slim spokes grouped into five pairs run from the rim deep into the centre. The strong concave makes the wheel look sculpted, especially on a wide rear axle. The photo shows a dark graphite finish.

Diameters 18-24″, widths 8-13J. Our fitment guide lists the EH172 for the Porsche 911 (992) Turbo, Cayenne, Macan, Ferrari SF90, 812 Superfast and other cars.
