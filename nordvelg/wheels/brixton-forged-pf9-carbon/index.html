About the PF9 Carbon+

The centre is forged with five sharp spokes, each splitting into a Y. The barrel is carbon fibre, and its weave shows around the edge of the wheel. It looks like a racing part even in plain black, as in the photo.

A carbon barrel makes the wheel more complex and more expensive than a regular forged one, so we only price it for a specific car and size. Brixton's production time is 7-10 weeks. Before you order, we send a sketch and check fitment.

How ordering works

  1. You send the make, model and year of your car
  2. We choose size, offset and finish and send a sketch
  3. You pay a 50% deposit, the factory makes the wheels
  4. You pay the balance and the wheels ship to you in Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania or Finland
