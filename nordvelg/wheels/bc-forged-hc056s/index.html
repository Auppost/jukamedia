About the HC056

Five Y-shaped spoke pairs on a separate barrel with a ring of bolts. You can polish the barrel and keep the centre matte, which gives the wheel more depth. The photo shows everything in polished silver.

Two-piece, 18-23″ diameters, 8-14J widths. There are HC056 and HC056S versions, shown below. Our fitment guide lists it for the Ferrari 296 GTB, Purosangue and 12Cilindri.
