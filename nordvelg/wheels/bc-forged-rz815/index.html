About the RZ815

Many slim spokes fan out from the centre and curve slightly towards the rim. The pattern is busy without looking heavy, and it suits large diameters well. The photo shows a grey metallic finish.

Sizes are 17-24″ with widths of 5-13J, and the factory states clearance for brakes up to 380 mm. Our fitment guide lists the RZ815 for the Porsche 911 (992), Cayenne, Macan and dozens of other cars, see below.
