About the HCS04

Slim spokes arranged in pairs on a separate barrel. In the polished version shown in the photo the wheel shines like chrome, but a calm matte finish is just as possible.

Two-piece, 18-23″ diameters, 8-14J widths. There are HCS04 and HCS04S versions, pictured below, and we explain the difference during the fitment check. It suits saloons and coupés that call for a clean, expensive look.
