About the TR08 Circuit+

A spoke mesh on the centre, a barrel with exposed bolts and a centre-lock nut in the middle, just like a race car. It is a three-piece wheel, so width and offset can be specified very precisely. The photo shows a dark centre with a matte barrel.

Centre-lock fits cars that have it from the factory, such as the Porsche 911 GT3 and GT3 RS. Our fitment guide lists the TR08 Circuit+ for the Porsche 911 (992), Cayenne, Macan and other models, and we confirm the fixing for your car. Brixton's production time is 7-10 weeks.
