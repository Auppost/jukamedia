About the HCA162

Ten slim straight spokes that taper slightly towards the rim, on a separate barrel with a lip. A light, restrained pattern that does not fight the car's design. The photo shows a pale champagne finish.

Two-piece, 18-23″ diameters, 8-14J widths. The HCA162 and HCA162S versions differ in details, compare the photos below. Our fitment guide lists this wheel for the Porsche 911 (992), Cayenne, Macan and other models.
