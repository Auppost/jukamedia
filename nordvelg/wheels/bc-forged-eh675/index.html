About the EH675

The spokes cross several times on their way to the rim, creating a dense, technical mesh. The pattern holds attention on cars with aggressive bodywork. The photo shows gloss black.

Diameters 18-23″, widths 8-13J. Our fitment guide lists the EH675 for the Lamborghini Temerario. For other cars we show how it looks on a sketch made from a photo of your car.
