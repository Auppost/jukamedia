BC Forged KL series

The BC Forged KL series has 17 monoblock designs. Diameters run from 18 to 24″ and widths from 5 to 13J, and every wheel is machined to your car's bolt pattern, centre bore and offset. A set of four costs from €4,760 incl. VAT and delivery. KL01, KL52 and KL54 have their own pages.

Good to know

  • Forged 6061-T6 aluminium billet, each wheel machined for your car: bolt pattern, centre bore and offset
  • Tested to the Japanese JWL and VIA strength standards
  • About 40 finishes, two can be combined
  • Made in 4-6 weeks, then delivered to Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania or Finland

The fitment check is free: first a sketch and an exact price, then you decide

How ordering works

  1. You send the make, model and year of your car
  2. We choose size, offset and finish and send a sketch
  3. You pay a 50% deposit, the factory makes the wheels
  4. You pay the balance and the wheels ship to you in Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania or Finland
