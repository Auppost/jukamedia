About the FDE11

Many slim straight spokes run from the centre almost to the rim edge. A clean, light looking pattern that keeps the brakes on show. The photo shows a dark metallic finish.

Brixton builds every set for a specific car, so we price it once size, width and finish are chosen. Production takes 7-10 weeks. If you are deciding between the FDE11 and similar BC Forged models, we can show both on a sketch.

How ordering works

  1. You send the make, model and year of your car
  2. We choose size, offset and finish and send a sketch
  3. You pay a 50% deposit, the factory makes the wheels
  4. You pay the balance and the wheels ship to you in Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania or Finland
