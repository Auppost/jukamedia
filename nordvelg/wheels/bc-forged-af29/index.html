About the AF29

The spokes branch and curve, and a row of small windows ties them together near the rim. It is an intricate, lace-like design that shows best in a polished or light finish, as in the photo.

Diameters 18-24″, widths 8-13J. This is a design for people who want their wheels to be noticed. We show how it looks on your car in a sketch before you pay.
