About the KL01

Five spokes, each splitting into two slim branches towards the rim. There is a lot of open space between them, so the caliper and brake disc stay fully visible. The photo shows a brushed matte silver, but any finish from the range is available.

The KL01 comes in 18 to 24″ diameters, with the width chosen for your arch from 5 to 13J. According to the factory, the spokes clear big brake kits with discs up to 380 mm. It suits the Porsche GT3 RS and other cars with large ceramic brakes.
